About Me

Hi! My name is Eliana Navejas. I'm located in downtown Kenosha and I graduated from Gateway of Racine Nail School. I have been doing nails for the past 2 years. I have a diverse portfolio, ranging from new trendy and advanced designs to simplistic and classic designs.

I offer manicures and pedicures along with nail extensions, using builder gel and acrylic extensions. I am so extremely grateful to be able to provide confidence through the beauty of your nails.

Always appreciating the support and love from clients like you!
